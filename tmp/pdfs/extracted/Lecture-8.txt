4.1 The Investment Dilemma
Imagine you have Rs. 1,00,000 to invest in the stock market. You are considering three
different types of stocks:
1.Blue-chip stocks(e.g., Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank)
2.Cryptocurrency or Small-cap stocks(e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum, Penny stocks)
3.Utility stocks(e.g., PowerGrid, NTPC, Adani Power)
The Question
Which stock should you invest in? Is it possible to make an informed decision just
by looking at the data?
Key Considerations:
•Risk vs Reward:Higher potential returns usually come with higher risk
•Consistency:Some stocks provide stable, predictable returns
•Extreme Events:Some stocks have rare but massive gains or losses
•Investment Horizon:Long-term vs short-term investment goals
The Answer:Yes! By analyzing thedistributionof returns, we can understand
thekurtosisof each stock. This helps us identify:
•Which stock has the highest risk (leptokurtic - fat tails)
•Which stock is most stable (platykurtic - light tails)
•Which stock follows normal market behavior (mesokurtic - normal tails)

5.1 Types of Kurtosis
There are three main types of kurtosis:
5.1.1 Mesokurtic (β 2 = 3)
•Meaning:"Meso" means middle. This is the baseline—a normal bell-shaped
distribution.
•Tails:Medium tails. Extreme events happen at an "expected" rate.
•Stock Market Application:Blue-chip stocks like Reliance, TCS, S&P 500
•Investment Decision:Moderate risk, predictable returns. Suitable for long-term
investors.
Investment Insight: Mesokurtic Stocks
•Who should invest?Conservative investors seeking steady growth
•Expected Returns:8-12% per annum (moderate)
•Risk:Low to moderate
•Strategy:Buy and hold, long-term investment
•Kurtosis Value:β 2 ≈3.0
5.1.2 Leptokurtic (β 2 >3)
•Meaning:"Lepto" means thin and slender. The distribution has a higher, sharper
peak andfatter tails.
•Tails:Heavy tails. Higher probability of extreme values.
•Stock Market Application:Cryptocurrency, Small-cap stocks, Penny stocks
•Investment Decision:High risk, high reward. Potential for massive gains or
devastating losses.
Investment Insight: Leptokurtic Stocks
•Who should invest?Aggressive investors with high risk tolerance
•Expected Returns:Very high potential (50-100%+), but also high loss po-
tential
•Risk:Very high
•Strategy:Short-term trading, stop-loss orders, diversification
•Warning:Not suitable for risk-averse investors or retirement funds
•Kurtosis Value:β 2 >3.0
13/20

--- PAGE 15 ---
5.1.3 Platykurtic (β 2 <3)
•Meaning:"Platy" means broad and flat. The distribution has a lower, flatter
peak andthinner tails.
•Tails:Light tails. Fewer extreme values than expected.
•Stock Market Application:Utility stocks like PowerGrid, NTPC
•Investment Decision:Low risk, consistent returns. Safe investment.
Investment Insight: Platykurtic Stocks
•Who should invest?Risk-averse investors, retirees, income seekers
•Expected Returns:5-8% per annum (low but steady)
•Risk:Very low
•Strategy:Dividend investing, long-term holding
•Advantage:Minimal volatility, predictable income
•Kurtosis Value:β 2 <3.0

5.3 How to Identify Kurtosis from Data
Step-by-Step Guide to Identify Kurtosis in Stock Data:
1.Collect Data:Get daily returns for at least 252 trading days (1 year)
2.Calculate Statistics:Mean, Standard Deviation, Skewness, Kurtosis
3.Check Kurtosis Value:
•β 2 ≈3.0→Mesokurtic (Normal)
•β 2 >3.0→Leptokurtic (Fat tails - More risk)
•β 2 <3.0→Platykurtic (Light tails - Less risk)
4.Visualize:Create a histogram to see the shape
5.Make Decision:Based on your risk tolerance and investment goals
Python Code to Calculate Kurtosis
import numpy as np
from scipy.stats import kurtosis
# Example: Calculate kurtosis for stock returns
returns = [0.12, -0.08, 0.15, -0.22, 0.18] # Sample returns
# Calculate kurtosis (fisher=False gives Pearson’s beta2)
kurt_value = kurtosis(returns, fisher=False)
print(f"Kurtosis: {kurt_value:.2f}")
# If kurtosis $\approx$ 3.0: Mesokurtic
# If kurtosis > 3.0: Leptokurtic
# If kurtosis < 3.0: Platykurtic

10 Key Takeaways for CSE Students
•Karl Pearson’s Skewness:
–Sk = (Mean - Mode)/SD
–Alternative: 3(Mean - Median)/SD
–Sk > 0: Right-skewed, Sk < 0: Left-skewed, Sk = 0: Symmetric
•Bowley’s Skewness:
–Based on quartiles
–Range: -1 to +1
–More robust to outliers
•Kurtosis in Stock Market:
– Mesokurtic (β2 = 3):Blue-chip stocks, moderate risk
– Leptokurtic (β2 >3):Cryptocurrency, high risk
– Platykurtic (β2 <3):Utility stocks, low risk
