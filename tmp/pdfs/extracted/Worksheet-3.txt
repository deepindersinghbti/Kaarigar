Topic 1: Inclusion-Exclusion Principle
1. The probability that an American industry will locate in Shanghai, China,
is 0.7, the probability that it will locate in Beijing, China, is 0.4, and the
probability that it will locate in either Shanghai or Beijing or both is 0.8.
What is the probability that the industry will locate
(a)in both cities?
(b)in neither city?
Answer: (a) 0.3, (b) 0.2
2. From past experience, a stockbroker believes that under present economic
conditions a customer will invest in tax-free bonds with a probability of
0.6, will invest in mutual funds with a probability of 0.3, and will invest in
both tax-free bonds and mutual funds with a probability of 0.15. At this
time, find the probability that a customer will invest
(a)in either tax-free bonds or mutual funds;
(b)in neither tax-free bonds nor mutual funds.
Answer: (a) 0.75, (b) 0.25
