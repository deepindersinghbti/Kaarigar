Practice Worksheet-1: Measures ofCentral Tendancy (Unit-I)
Section A: Arithmetic Mean (Discrete Data)
1. The number of new orders received by a company over the last 25 working
days were recorded as follows: 3, 0, 1, 4, 4, 4, 2, 5, 3, 6, 4, 5, 1, 4, 2, 3,
0, 2, 0, 5, 4, 2, 3, 3, 1. Calculate the arithmetic mean for the number of
orders received over all similar working days.
Answer: 2.84 orders
2. The human resource manager at a city hospital began a study of the over-
time hours of the registered nurses. Fifteen nurses were selected at random,
and following overtime hours during a month were recorded:
13 13 12 15 7 15 5 12 6 7 12 10 9 13 12
5 9 6 10 5 6 9 6 9 12
Compute the arithmetic mean of overtime hours during the month.
Answer: 9.52 hours
Section B: Arithmetic Mean (Continuous Data)
1. A company is planning to improve plant safety. For this, accident data for
the last 50 weeks was compiled. These data are grouped into the frequency
distribution as shown below. Calculate the A.M. of the number of accidents
per week.
Number of accidents 0–4 5–9 10–14 15–19 20–24
Number of weeks 5 22 13 8 2
Answer: 10 accidents per week
2. The following distribution gives the pattern of overtime work done by 100
employees of a company. Calculate the average overtime work done per
employee (using shortcut/step-deviation method).
Overtime hours 10–15 15–20 20–25 25–30 30–35 35–40
Number of employees 11 20 35 20 8 6
Answer: 23.1 hours

Section C: Correcting the Mean
1.
(a)The average dividend declared by a group of 10 chemical companies was 18 per
cent. Later on, it was discovered that one correct figure, 12, was misread as 22.
Find the correct average dividend.
Answer: 17%

tions were misread as 92 and 8 instead of 192 and 88. Find the correct mean.
Answer: 50.9

Section E: Median (Grouped Data)
1. In a factory employing 3000 persons, 5 per cent earn less than Rs 150 per
day, 580 earn from Rs 151 to Rs 200 per day, 30 per cent earn from Rs 201
to Rs 250 per day, 500 earn from Rs 251 to Rs 300 per day, 20 per cent
earn from Rs 301 to Rs 350 per day, and the rest earn Rs 351 or more per
day. What is the median wage?
Answer: Rs 243.28
