8 Key Takeaways
Key Points to Remember:
1.First Central Moment (µ1)is always 0 - use it as a check for your calculations
2.Second Central Moment (µ 2)gives variance - square root gives standard
deviation
3.ThirdCentralMoment(µ 3)givesskewness-indicatessymmetry/asymmetry
4.Fourth Central Moment (µ4)gives kurtosis - indicates tail thickness
5.Integer mean makes calculations cleaner- choose data carefully to get
integer mean
6.Always interpret the results:
•What does the mean tell us about the average?
•What does standard deviation tell us about spread?
•What does skewness tell us about the direction of outliers?
•What does kurtosis tell us about extreme values?
